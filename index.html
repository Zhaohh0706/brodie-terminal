§05

Fee flow & buybacks

The pool charges 1%. Fees accrue at the hook, a bot address swaps them into ETH, and a splitter pays two recipients in a fixed 70 / 30 split: 70% to the creator fee recipient, 30% to the Pons protocol Safe. Below is the measured flow — including how much of it actually came back as buying.

§11

Migration V1 → V2

Only official migration route: ponsfamily.com/migrate. Anyone asking you to send V1 tokens to a personal address, or asking for a seed phrase, is a scammer. Migration is an on-chain swap, not a transfer.

How it actually worked: migration was not a 1:1 burn-swap and it did not run through the v4 migration pool. Depositors called deposit(uint256) on the migration contract at 0x9310bc8b…, which logged a credit at that epoch's rate — five epochs capped at 300M / 280M / 210M / 140M / 70M, paying 100 / 80 / 60 / 40 / 20%, so 1,000,000,000 V1 became 720,000,000 of credits. The deposited V1 was sold back into the old 1% pool for ETH, which seeded the ETH side of the new v4 pool; the V2 is only minted when a depositor calls claim(), and minting stays open until 2026-12-21. No V1 was ever destroyed; its totalSupply is still exactly 1,000,000,000.

About 7.8% of credits have not been claimed yet. The migration contract records each deposit as a credit; the V2 is only minted when the depositor calls claim(). As of the block shown above, roughly 56M of credits are still unclaimed. That is not a missing reserve — minting is on demand, within MAX_SUPPLY — but the mint window closes for good on 2026-12-21 16:47 UTC, after which anyone can call renounceMinter() and unclaimed credits are lost. If you deposited, claim before then.

Origin

14 Jul V1 opens. The WETH pool for 0x45F8… goes live. The original dev later dumps the accumulated fees and V1's credibility with them.
29 Aug V2 opens. The PONS team takes over. 0x7370… launches a BRODIE/ETH pool on Uniswap v4 and V1 → V2 migration goes live at ponsfamily.com.

The community line is "Not a gorillion. A Brodillion." It is a joke, but it describes the pricing mechanism accurately: nothing here is priced off cash flow. It is priced off whether the provenance is unique and how many people keep agreeing that it matters. Both of those can evaporate inside a week.
