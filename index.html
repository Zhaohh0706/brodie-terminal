Burn engine

—

The vault is a contract with no owner. It can’t be paused, upgraded or emptied. It collects the creator’s 70% of pool fees, buys BRODIE in the same pool and calls burn(), so total supply goes down. The other 30% of every fee is Pons’ protocol share, set by Pons, not by us. Everything here is read straight from the contract, whose source is verified on Blockscout ↗.

Market & flow

How deep the pool is, and how busy trading has been.

Supply ledger

V2 supply changes in two ways: the migration contract mints when someone claims, and burn() destroys. Minted, supply, credits and the deadline are read live, and burned is counted from the token’s own burn events. Each burn is listed on the Burn tab.

Fee flow & buybacks

The pool charges a 1% fee. Fees are converted to ETH and split 70 / 30: 70% to the creator recipient, 30% to the Pons protocol. Since 24 September the creator recipient is the burn vault, so that 70% now goes to buybacks and burns.

Brodie’s story

In August 2016, Robinhood’s support account replied to a customer with a photo of their office dog, Brodie. It was a throwaway tweet.

Ten years later, after Robinhood had gone public and launched its own chain, someone found the tweet and turned Brodie into a coin. There have been ups and downs and a few changes of hands since. Brodie is still here.

The next chapter is on-chain. A vault with no owner takes the creator’s 70% of trading fees, buys BRODIE back and burns it, about once a day.

"We're a dog-friendly company as well… Check out our office dog, Brodie." @AskRobinhood · 4 August 2016

2026 Found again. @MEADGod, founder of Pons, digs up the tweet ten years later.
14 Jul V1 opens. The WETH pool for 0x45F8… goes live.
29 Aug V2 launches. V2 goes live on Pons with a BRODIE/ETH pool on Uniswap v4. V1 holders can migrate at ponsfamily.com.
22 Sep Burn vault deployed. A contract with no owner. It receives fees and can only use them to buy BRODIE back and burn it.
24 Sep Handover. The creator fee recipient is switched to the vault. From now on the creator’s 70% of fees is burned.
24 Sep Burn #1. The vault buys 48,683 BRODIE with 0.006 ETH and burns it. Nobody signed anything.
Next About once a day. At a random time, for as long as fees keep coming in.
